# Results

Model selection and held-out performance at the **1.4× hydrolysis control**.
Every number comes from `results/` and is regenerated by `./run_all.sh`.

## Data and split

1085 acyl chloride / amine pairs (31 acyl chlorides, 35 amines), 370 with a
fitted rate constant. A pair is *reactive* if a rate constant was measured or
the reaction was too fast to resolve, and *un-reactive* if the acyl chloride
was consumed by a competing pathway first.

Whole reactants are held out: acyl chlorides 2, 18, 25 and amines 18, 21, 23.

### How the held-out reactants were chosen

The test reactants are chosen by a fixed, class-stratified rule
(`prism_ml.splits.build_split`), with no random draw. It is applied
separately to the acyl chlorides and to the amines:

1. **Group by chemical class.** Each reactant takes the `class` column of
   `PRISM_substrate_features.xlsx`.
2. **Decide how many to take from each class, by its size:**
   - 2 members or fewer: none. The class stays wholly in training, so that
     leave-one-reactant-out can still hold one member out while a sibling
     remains in training.
   - 3–10 members: one.
   - more than 10 members: two.
3. **Pick typical members, not extremes.** Members are sorted by how many
   measurable reactions they have (ties broken by id). One pick is the
   median; two picks are the members one third and two thirds of the way
   along.
4. **Exclusions.** Amine 34 is never held out, because its descriptors came
   from a different pipeline from every other reactant's. It still counts
   towards the size of its class.

| reactant | class label (members) | held out (measurable reactions) | stays in training |
|---|---|---|---|
| acyl chloride | `aromatic` (19) | **2** benzoyl chloride (17), **18** 4-methylbenzoyl chloride (10) | — |
| acyl chloride | `aliphatic` (8) | **25** dodecanoyl chloride (8) | — |
| acyl chloride | `hetero` (2), `mixture` (2) | — | 7, 20; 21, 27 |
| amine | `1_aromatic` (22) | **18** 3-aminoacetophenone (7), **21** 2-(2-methoxyethoxy)aniline (19) | — |
| amine | `2_aliphatic` (8) | **23** 2,6-dimethylpiperidine (14) | — |
| amine | `1_aliphatic` (2), `1_hetero` (1), `1_mixture` (1), `2_mixture` (1) | — | 1, 22; 17; 29; 32 |

Amine class labels start with 1 for primary and 2 for secondary amines.

Every pair containing a held-out reactant goes to the test set. That gives
three regimes: unseen acyl chloride (96 pairs), unseen amine (84) and both
unseen (9).

| set | pairs | with a rate constant |
|---|---|---|
| training pool | 896 | 298 |
| held-out test (unseen acyl / unseen amine / both unseen) | 189 (96 / 84 / 9) | 72 |

Model selection uses only the pool, by leave-one-reactant-out (LORO)
cross-validation: 100 folds (28 acyl, 32 amine, 40 pair). Each candidate is
scored by **joint = ½ accuracy + ½ R²** (R² = 1 − SS_res/SS_tot on log₁₀ *k*,
macro-averaged over regimes).

## 1. Descriptor set (`results/benchmark/feature_sets/`)

Gradient-boosted trees (histogram) on each descriptor tier:

| tier | descriptors | CV accuracy | CV R² | CV MAE | joint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 2 (reduced)** | 52 | 0.737 | **0.562** | **0.337** | **0.650** |
| Tier 3 | 72 | 0.742 | 0.545 | 0.339 | 0.644 |
| Tier 2 | 56 | 0.734 | 0.552 | 0.340 | 0.643 |
| Tier 3 (reduced) | 68 | 0.735 | 0.544 | 0.342 | 0.639 |
| Tier 1 | 34 | **0.751** | 0.479 | 0.377 | 0.615 |

- Tier 1 is geometry only (buried volume, Sterimol, SASA, pyramidalisation,
  dispersion).
- Tier 2 adds one xTB single point.
- Tier 2 (reduced) drops electron affinity and hardness.
- Tier 3 adds Fukui indices.

Tier 1 classifies about as well as the others, but the electronic descriptors
influence reactivity based on the hammett analysis. Tier 2 (reduced) is selected.

the best regressor.

## Chemical space (`analysis/results/chemical_space/`)

`python analysis/chemical_space.py`. Each reactant is encoded as a Morgan
fingerprint (radius 2, 2048 bits) and compared by Tanimoto similarity, over
all reactants in the labelled reactions and the held-out split.

For acyl chlorides, the C(=O)Cl group they all share is left out of the
fingerprints, so they compare by their R groups. With the group kept, it
inflates similarity: the mean pairwise similarity is 0.292 with it and 0.130
without.

Figures:
- similarity heatmaps of all reactants, train | test;
- pairwise diversity (1 − similarity) of acyl chlorides and amines, overlaid;
- pairwise similarity distributions;
- each reactant's similarity to its nearest training reactant.

| | acyl chlorides | amines |
|---|---|---|
| reactants (train / test) | 31 (28 / 3) | 35 (32 / 3) |
| functional classes / Murcko scaffolds | 7 / 7 | 4 / 12 |
| mean pairwise diversity (1 − similarity) | 0.870 ± 0.129 | 0.855 ± 0.130 |
| mean similarity: train–train / test–train | 0.122 / 0.169 | 0.147 / 0.139 |
| nearest training reactant: test (mean) | 0.653 | 0.491 |
| nearest training reactant: training, leave-one-out (mean) | 0.336 | 0.430 |

The test reactants are at least as close to training as training reactants
are to each other. The held-out split therefore tests unseen reactants inside
the library's chemical space, not extrapolation beyond it.

Radius-2 fingerprints do not distinguish alkyl chain lengths beyond a few
carbons, so two homologue pairs have identical fingerprints:
- acyl chlorides 13 and 25 (heptanoyl and dodecanoyl chloride; 25 is a test
  reactant);
- amines 7 and 10 (4-heptyl- and 4-hexylaniline).

## 4. Deployed model (`model/`)

The model in `model/` is trained on **all 1085 reactions**. Its descriptors are
computed from SMILES by `prism_ml.descriptors`, the same pipeline
`scripts/predict.py` runs on an unseen molecule.

The stored descriptor table (`PRISM_substrate_features.xlsx`, used for
sections 1–3) came from a different conformer workflow. For several acyl
chloride descriptors it does not agree with the SMILES pipeline. For example,
SMILES-computed C=O carbon charge correlates only r = 0.28 with the stored
value, so a model trained on the table would not transfer to unseen molecules.

Before the final fit, the same pipeline is validated on the held-out split:

| | accuracy | precision | recall | rate R² | rate MAE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SMILES-descriptor pipeline, held-out test | 0.847 | 0.840 | 0.810 | 0.803 | 0.265 |

These numbers are also stored in `model/metadata.json`.

## Reproducibility

- The histogram learners (HistGB, LightGBM) give identical scores on every
  machine tried.
- Exact-split learners (exact gradient boosting, AdaBoost) can differ in the
  third decimal between CPU types.
- The graph networks train on a GPU. They reproduce exactly on the same GPU
  (NVIDIA A30), but can differ by about 0.015 in joint score on other
  hardware.
